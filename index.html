About Us

Kandhasamy Public Charitable Trust (KPCT) is a non-profit organization focused on education, healthcare, and community development. We support underprivileged children, empower women, and provide essential services to build a better and self-sustaining society.

Mission

KPCT is dedicated to empowering underprivileged communities by providing education, healthcare, and sustainable development opportunities. The trust aims to create a positive impact by bridging social and economic gaps, ensuring that every individual has access to essential resources for a dignified life.

Vision

KPCT envisions a society where every person has equal access to education and healthcare, leading to self-reliant and empowered communities. The trust strives to eliminate barriers to growth and development, fostering a future where people can thrive without limitations.

Achievements

Education

KPCT provides free tuition, scholarships, and digital literacy programs to ensure quality education for underprivileged children, empowering them for a better future.

Healthcare

The trust conducts free medical camps, health check-ups, and awareness programs on hygiene, nutrition, and mental health, ensuring overall community well-being.

Community Development

KPCT supports rural development through sanitation projects, clean water initiatives, environmental programs, and women's skill training, creating a self-sustaining society.
