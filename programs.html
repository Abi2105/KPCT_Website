Our Programs

Transforming lives through education, healthcare, and community empowerment

Education Program

📚

Empowering Through Quality Education

KPCT is committed to providing underprivileged children with access to quality education through comprehensive support and innovative learning approaches.

Program Components:

  • Free tuition and coaching for underprivileged children
  • Educational scholarships and financial assistance
  • Digital literacy and computer training programs
  • Mentorship and career guidance for students
  • School supplies and educational materials provision
  • Support for school infrastructure development

Healthcare Services

⚕️

Building Healthier Communities

Through preventive and curative healthcare initiatives, KPCT ensures that quality medical care and health awareness reach the most vulnerable communities.

Program Components:

  • Free medical camps and health check-ups
  • Health awareness and disease prevention programs
  • Nutrition and hygiene education initiatives
  • Mental health support and counseling services
  • Emergency medical assistance and support
  • Maternal and child health initiatives

Women Empowerment

👩‍💼

Strengthening Women's Independence

KPCT recognizes the critical role of women in community development and provides comprehensive support for their economic and social empowerment.

Program Components:

  • Skill development and vocational training
  • Self-help groups and cooperative formation
  • Financial literacy and microfinance support
  • Entrepreneurship and business training
  • Gender sensitization and women's rights awareness
  • Income generation and livelihood programs

Community Development

🌍

Building Sustainable Communities

KPCT works with communities to address fundamental development challenges and create conditions for sustainable growth and self-reliance.

Program Components:

  • Sanitation and clean water initiatives
  • Environmental conservation and afforestation
  • Disaster relief and rehabilitation support
  • Community infrastructure development
  • Social welfare support for vulnerable groups
  • Community organizing and mobilization

Livelihood Programs

💼

Creating Economic Opportunities

KPCT supports individuals and families to build sustainable livelihoods through skill development, business training, and market linkages.

Program Components:

  • Skill-based vocational training programs
  • Job placement assistance and support
  • Entrepreneurship development and mentoring
  • Access to credit and financial services
  • Market linkage and value chain support
  • Business incubation and support services

Social Welfare

🤝

Supporting Vulnerable Populations

KPCT provides targeted support to elderly, disabled, and other vulnerable populations to ensure dignified living and social inclusion.

Program Components:

  • Support for elderly and aged populations
  • Assistance for persons with disabilities
  • Orphan and vulnerable children support
  • Shelter and housing assistance
  • Food security and nutrition programs
  • Legal aid and advocacy services
