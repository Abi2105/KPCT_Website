KPCT - Making a Difference

Kandhasamy Public Charitable Trust (KPCT) is committed to bridging the gap between privilege and underprivilege by providing equitable access to education, healthcare, and livelihood opportunities.

Since our establishment, we have touched the lives of thousands through our focused initiatives in education, healthcare, community development, and women empowerment.
